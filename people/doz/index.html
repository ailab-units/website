About

First-year PhD student working in a probabilistic framework, on inference and gradient-based optimization of probabilistic programs — a neuro-explicit approach to generative modeling where program structure and uncertainty are made explicit rather than learned as a black box. Background in Physics (BSc) and Data Science and Scientific Computing (MSc). Also interested in explainable AI and causality.
