Gaia Saveri

Research Collaborator · PhD (national AI programme)
neuro-explicit methodsformal methodsexplainabilityLLM

About

Builds neuro-explicit representations: continuous, invertible embeddings of Signal Temporal Logic formulae that let generative models be steered and constrained by explicit symbolic specifications, rather than learning them implicitly. Also works on concept-based explainability for time series (the STELLE framework, with Irene Ferfoglia and Simone Silvetti), making model decisions traceable back to human-readable logical concepts.
