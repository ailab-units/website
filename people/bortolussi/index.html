Luca Bortolussi

Principal Investigator · Full Professor
formal methodsneuro-symbolic AIstochastic systems

About

Leads the AI Lab. His work spans two decades, from mean-field approximation and stochastic model checking to today's neuro-symbolic generative AI and adversarial robustness. He supervises most of the lab's PhD students and holds long-running collaborations with the University of Edinburgh, Saarland University, and King's College London.
