Emanuele Ballarin

PhD Candidate · also affiliated with IIT Genova
trustworthy GenAIadversarial robustnessphysics-inspired ML

About

Best known for CARSO, an adversarial defence mechanism combining adversarial training and purification — part of the lab's broader effort to make deep generative and discriminative models trustworthy under attack, not just accurate on clean data. Also a co-author of RogueAI, probing whether conversational AI can be trusted to be honest. His PhD thesis is broader still: it covers continuous-time quantum walks, Koopman operator learning, and the Forward-Forward algorithm as a biologically-plausible alternative to backpropagation.
