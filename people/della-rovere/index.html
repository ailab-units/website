About

My research is in the Electronic Design Automation field, with a focus on analog integrated circuit layout automation. My current research combines reinforcement learning for device placement, sequential pathfinding for net routing, and tree search extensions for whole-layout optimization.

Going forward, I plan to explore LLM agents in the loop and surrogate models for post-layout simulation, with the aim of building AI-based tools for a field that remains largely unexplored.

Beyond the lab, I am an enthusiast of all things science and technology.
